# Salesforce Q4 FY26 logo and segment icons

Source: `input/processed/salesforce-q4-fy26.png` (2667 × 1500); recipe
`input/icon-crop-specs/salesforce-q4-fy26-raster.json`.
Seven crops replace the hand-drawn SVG logo and segment icons: the company
cloud, Sales, Service, Slack (Platform, Slack & Other), Marketing & Commerce,
MuleSoft and Tableau (Integration & Analytics). Each crop is the tight subject
box plus 2 px; the Marketing & Commerce crop masks the overlapping "M" of its
segment label. Source labels, flows, node faces, publisher branding and URLs
are excluded. The extractor validates isolation, margins and centering for all
seven crops; runtime outputs are compressed local PNGs, and every annotation
declares `clearance: true` (A6 passed in en and zh). Asset acceptance remains
pending human review.
